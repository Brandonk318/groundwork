# Groundwork 33-step reentry checklist (draft, Brann 2026-10-29)
Housing/food steps are resource pointers only (211, local resources). Groundwork does not provide housing/food and does not partner with providers.
## Before release
1. Order your birth certificate
2. Request your Social Security card
3. Start the state ID application
4. Set goals with your case manager
5. Plan your ride home
6. Line up one support contact
## First 48 hours
7. Find a safe place to stay (call 211 or local housing resources)
8. Find food for this week (call 211 or a local food pantry)
9. Check your release papers and deadlines
10. Confirm your first check-in date and conditions
11. Save 988 and 911 in your phone
## First week
12. Get your state ID
13. Get a phone, email and free internet access
14. Put every court date and check-in on a calendar
15. Contact local reentry services
## Legal and supervision
16. Know your supervision conditions
17. Check your fines, fees and restitution
18. Check your driver's license status
## Health
19. Enroll in Medicaid, and SNAP if you need it
20. Get your health records
21. Make a medication plan
22. Book a doctor's appointment
23. Book a mental health appointment
24. Get a substance use evaluation
25. Find a support group
## Work
26. Build your resume
27. Apply to second-chance employers
28. Look into training or certificates
## Stability
29. Open a bank account and make a budget
30. Start rebuilding your credit
31. Reconnect with family, and handle child support if it applies
32. Set a daily routine
33. Set one long-term goal and celebrate your 90-day milestone
