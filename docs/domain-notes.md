# Domain notes

## Who's building it
Brann is a licensed insurance agent and self-employed business owner in Fort Worth, TX. He builds AI-powered platforms for contractors, roofing, insurance and the trades (Empire/Momoempire, Mind At Ease Solutions, Trade Circle). This app is a **separate** project from all of them, though it can reuse code patterns (Trade Circle) and the Contractor Academy video and quiz engine.

## Audience
- People recently released from incarceration (returning citizens).
- People in substance-use recovery, including people who were never incarcerated.
- One community, one feed, one identity. Never label a member by which path they came in on unless they choose to share it.

## Texas trades and licensing (TDLR), for the work and micro-gig angle
- **Licensed trades pushed for TDCJ releases:** cosmetology, electrical, and ACR (air conditioning and refrigeration). These need TDLR licensing, and TDLR runs a criminal-history review. Members can ask for a criminal history evaluation before they invest in training.
- **No state license needed:** roofing, general contracting, painting, landscaping, carpentry and concrete. These are the fastest on-ramps, and the best fit for the micro-gig board and Brann's contractor network.
- **Bonding is a gap:** standard surety bonds screen out felonies. That blocks some contracting work. It's an open product and partner question. Brann's insurance license is relevant here, and so is the federal bonding program, which should be checked before anything about it is shown in the app.
- **Parole conditions** can restrict self-employment for some members. Any self-employment, gig or business feature has to say "check with your parole or probation officer" and must never assume a member is allowed to take gig work.
- Local cities may require registration or permits even for unlicensed trades. Verify each city before it shows up in the app.

## Content rules
- Use person-first language: "returning citizen", "person in recovery". Never "ex-con", "inmate" or "addict".
- Legal, tax and medical content is general information, not advice. Point members to legal aid, counselors and 988.
- The resource directory covers **legal aid, treatment and transit**. It explicitly does **not** cover food or housing, because that space is already crowded.
